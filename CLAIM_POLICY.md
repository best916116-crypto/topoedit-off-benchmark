# Claim policy

## Recommended manuscript title

TopoEdit-Off: a source-aware benchmark and validation-priority framework for high-mismatch genome-editing off-target candidates

## Supported claims

- Source-aware harmonization is required for cross-assay off-target candidate benchmarking.
- TOPO and base-editor observed-positive spaces are high-mismatch dominated relative to conventional CRISPRoffT positives.
- Low-mismatch sequence-centric prioritization under-recovers TOPO/BE observed positives.
- Mismatch-6 expansion creates a large candidate space and is not itself a ranking solution.
- A strict validation-panel resource can be constructed from the benchmark.

## Conditional or supplement-only claims

- AI all-PU provides experimental expanded-panel enrichment only.
- TOPO-PU is not promoted as a global predictor and requires review/retraining.
- ATMP is retained as exploratory context only.

## Forbidden claims

- Calibrated off-target probability predictor.
- ATMP predicts off-target probability.
- Multi-atlas or AI rank replaces the default/sequence baseline.
- BE-specific AI head is successful.
