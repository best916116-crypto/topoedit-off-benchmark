# Claim policy

Supported:

- Source-aware label harmonization is required for cross-assay benchmark construction.
- TOPO-seq and base-editor observed-positive spaces in this benchmark are high-mismatch dominated relative to conventional nuclease positives.
- Size-matched observed-positive recovery is used for top-K ranking evaluation.
- Validation panels are prioritization resources for downstream experimental planning.

Not claimed:

- TopoEdit-Off is not a calibrated universal off-target probability predictor.
- Unlabeled candidates are not experimentally validated negatives.
- The validation-priority panels are not wet-validated panels.
